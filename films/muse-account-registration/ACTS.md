# ACTS.md — How to register for a Muse account

A how-to lecture film: Bear's actual registration flow for a Muse account,
rebuilt from his screenshots — fresh email, a $10 merchant-locked virtual
card from privacy.com, Instagram account, age check, muse.ai signup.

**Exact title:** How to register for a Muse account

**Core promise:** By the end, the viewer can replicate the flow: an email
Meta has never seen, a virtual card locked to Meta with a $10 limit, the
Instagram prerequisite, the $1 age check, the 6-digit code, the disclosure —
and understands the reusable pattern (merchant-locked card, small limit)
for any signup that demands a credit card.

**Structure:** Four acts.

- Act I — The setup (2 beats): the fresh email; the $10 locked card.
- Act II — The chain (3 beats): Instagram first; the $1 age check; why the
  chain isolates the blast radius.
- Act III — The registration (4 beats): muse.ai login; the 6-digit code;
  the disclosure (read it); you're in.
- Act IV — The pattern (1 beat): the reusable privacy pattern.

**Tone:** practical, slightly mischievous. This is a privacy playbook, not a
product ad. The hero is the $10 leash.

**What this film is not:** not an endorsement of Meta's data practices; not
a review of Muse. It's a registration walkthrough with the privacy hygiene
built in.

**Privacy rule for this film:** replicate the screenshots, but exclude
private information. Shown: muse@humanitarians.ai (Bear's org email, shared
by him in the screenshots). Excluded: his personal email, his full name as
displayed on the privacy.com dashboard, any card numbers (none appeared).

**Source facts:**
- Email created: muse@humanitarians.ai (Bear's words + screenshot of the
  code-entry page showing the code was sent there).
- privacy.com: new merchant-locked card named "Meta CC", locked to merchant
  Meta, $10 limit (Bear's words + screenshots of the card-creation UI).
- Instagram account created because Muse signup requires it (Bear's words).
- Age confirmation: $1.00 charged, refunded in 5–7 business days (screenshot).
- muse.ai: email login, 6-digit code, disclosure page ("Can take actions for
  you" / "Works around the clock" / "Stay in control"), chat greeting:
  "Hey! I'm your personal agent, not just a regular assistant."
- The GitHub PAT screenshot Bear sent belongs to a different task (token
  provisioning) and is not part of this flow — excluded.
