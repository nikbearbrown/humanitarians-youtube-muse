# ACTS.md — Muse widens the search

Assignment 2 appendix film: how Muse would do the seven-board sweep — three
ATSs, 643 postings, the SmartRecruiters build, the Writer blurb failure, the
better fit, the changed search word, and what wasn't found.

**Exact title:** Muse widens the search

**Core promise:** By the end, the viewer knows why "one board is not a
search": the wider look found a better fit, changed the search word, and
documented exactly what the market doesn't have.

**Structure:** Four acts.

- Act I — Three systems (2 beats): the ATS table; building the
  SmartRecruiters reader.
- Act II — The field (2 beats): seven boards side by side; the Writer blurb
  failure.
- Act III — What changed (2 beats): the better fit; the changed word.
- Act IV — What it didn't find (2 beats): no part-time anywhere; the three
  unreadable companies.

**Tone:** field-research energy. Patient, specific, honest about cost.

**What this film is not:** not a scraper tutorial; not "more data is always
better." It's about what widening the search actually changed.

**Source facts** (from `assignment-2/part-1-appendix-seven-boards.md`):
- 643 postings, 7 companies (Figma, Writer, Canva, Miro, Webflow, Notion,
  Jasper), 3 ATSs, one afternoon.
- Greenhouse: Figma, Webflow, Miro — one call, full ad text.
- Ashby: Writer, Notion, Jasper — one call; names can have spaces.
- SmartRecruiters: Canva — paged 100 at a time, no ad text; 248 jobs took
  3m38s and 251 requests. The new work ("research the actual tech stack").
- Unreadable: Framer (hand-built pages), Adobe (Workday), Sketch/InVision/
  Mural (unconfirmed; InVision wound down design products in 2024).
- Writer: first pass flagged 51/51 (company blurb words); ignoring
  "generative AI", "AI agents", "learning" brought it to 24.
- Better fit: Webflow Senior Developer Educator — US remote, new,
  $113K–$155K, on camera, behind Webflow University.
- Changed word: "Educator", not "Advocate" — two of the six best matches
  never use "Advocate".
- Not found: any part-time/contract/consulting education role in the US at
  any of the seven.
