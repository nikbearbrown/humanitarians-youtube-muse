# ACTS.md — Muse finds the dream job

Assignment 2, Part 1 film: how Muse would do the dream-job assignment — the
Figma worked example. The method (machine ranks, human decides), the honest
override, the real gap, the real disappointment.

**Exact title:** Muse finds the dream job

**Core promise:** By the end, the viewer understands the assignment's real
lesson: the machine ranks 152 postings and the human overrules the top
scores — and why that override is the point, not a failure.

**Structure:** Four acts.

- Act I — The method (2 beats): the skill and the scheme; 152 → 49.
- Act II — The honest override (2 beats): the top scores were wrong; the
  human chose.
- Act III — The honesty (2 beats): the real gap; the real disappointment.
- Act IV — The answer (2 beats): Designer Advocate; the runner-up.

**Tone:** honest, dry. The film admires the method and the override equally.
No inspirational music.

**What this film is not:** not a Figma ad; not "AI found me a job." The
machine did the ranking; the person did the choosing.

**Source facts** (all from `assignment-2/part-1-dream-job.md` and
`assignment-2/evidence/`):
- `greenhouse-watch` skill against Figma's public Greenhouse board; scheme
  `bear-figma-0.1`; 152 open postings, raw response saved.
- 49 flagged relevant, 103 skipped. String-match rules between résumé fields
  and posting fields — no model judgment.
- Top scores (11.5): AI Applied Scientist, Forward Deployed Engineer,
  Marketing Engineer — full-time engineering; matched skills, not the goal.
- The choice: **Designer Advocate, Figma** — full-time, US hubs,
  $153K–$317K, posted 2026-09-01. Job: written, visual, video teaching
  material for the design community.
- The gap: never shipped a design system in Figma; the Madison project
  closes it. Plus public speaking and up to 25% travel.
- The disappointment: zero part-time/contract/freelance/consulting roles on
  the board; no Education/Learning/Curriculum/Content titles.
- Runner-up: Researcher, Figma Agentic Experiences — the one remote-OK role;
  wants 7+ years UX research.
